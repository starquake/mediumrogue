Water for F — four takes on the same lake

Everything else is F as approved (per-hex noise, borders-only outlines, grain, scatter, warm/cool tilt); only the water changes. W1 is what the earlier mockup shipped — the one that reads as stripes. Depth below means hex distance to the nearest non-water hex, computed once at map build from the same neighbour walk the borders-only outlines already need.

W1 · Regular bands — current

Even horizontal banding. Reads as a texture swatch laid over hexes rather than as water — the lines run straight through the shore and ignore the shape of the lake.

W2 · Depth shading

Darker toward the middle, lighter in the shallows at the shore. The lake gets a shape and a bottom. No texture at all — just a fill colour driven by depth.

W3 · Depth + glints

Depth shading plus sparse bright flecks, denser in the shallows. Reads as light catching a surface, and matches the speckle already used on grass.

W4 · Depth + shore ripples

Depth shading plus short broken arcs that follow the shoreline and fade with depth — the water reacts to the land it touches instead of ignoring it.
