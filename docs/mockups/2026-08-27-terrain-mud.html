mediumrogue — mud, the fifth terrain (#437)

Real generated terrain, not sketched: every hex below comes from GenerateMap at mudLevel 0.44 / mudMoisture 0.55, dumped from Go and drawn with the client's own palette (grass #35513a · forest #22391f · water #1d3d5c · rock #45443f), per-hex value noise and grain. So the placement, the patch shapes and the coverage are what you would actually get — only mud's colour is the open question.
Top row: three candidate mud colours over the same mud-rich ground. Bottom row: the leading candidate across three seeds, because mud varies a lot seed to seed (5%–14% of land) and that variance is worth seeing before it is signed off.
Each panel is centred on the muddiest neighbourhood in its world, not on an average one — so these are the wettest views each seed has to offer, and the whole-world percentage in each caption is the honest number.
