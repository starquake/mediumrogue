All four panels are radius 60 (10,981 hexes), drawn with the client's real palette (grass #35513a · forest #22391f · mud #4b3a27 · water #1d3d5c · rock #45443f). Panel A is the actual current generator, dumped from Go. Panels B–D are a real graph-first generator running in this page — nodes placed per ring, connected outward, corridors carved along the edges — not a drawing.
Detour × is the metric that matters: mean walking distance ÷ straight-line distance between sampled pairs. ×1.0 means you go straight there (open plain); higher means you route around things, which is what branching actually is. Mean degree is shown but is a POOR metric — a 7-hex-wide corridor is locally open too, so it barely moves. #458's table uses it and should be corrected.
